# Prompt vocabulary

Terms for lighting, lens, composition, material and print, each tied to a prompt in this repository that uses it. The quoted phrase is copied from the case prompt, and each case page shows the image it produced. All examples were tested on GPT Image 2.5. Other generators may read these terms differently.

## Lighting

- **Raking light.** Light at a shallow angle across a surface, which shows texture and seams. "Soft museum softbox light rakes the enamel" ([Cream Terminal That Never Existed](../cases/cream-terminal-that-never-existed-b3d4a9d3.md)). Also "sculptural side light raking across the cracks" ([Rust Seam Splitting a Gray Painted Face](../cases/rust-seam-splitting-a-gray-painted-face-7cabb9a6.md)).
- **Single light source.** One light that the whole scene depends on. "the single light source of the frame, underlighting her chin and cheeks" ([Girl Holding a Fallen Moon in a Tidal Pool](../cases/girl-holding-a-fallen-moon-in-a-tidal-pool-65b8e726.md)).
- **Blue hour.** The short time after sunset with deep blue light. "editorial product photograph at blue hour" ([Chrome Watering Can Pours a Silver Arc](../cases/chrome-watering-can-pours-a-silver-arc-579c8330.md)).
- **Warm against cool.** Two color temperatures in one frame. "Warm amber strip lights on the background columns contrast the cool body" ([Mint Concept Motorcycle in a Dark Garage](../cases/mint-concept-motorcycle-in-a-dark-garage-8b5688cf.md)). Also "Warm task lamps balance the cool blue city light" ([Blond Man Studies a Rainy City Model](../cases/blond-man-studies-a-rainy-city-model-9acedb62.md)).
- **Window light.** Soft light from one side with a stated direction. "Soft window light falls from the upper left, gentle and even" ([Felted Fox Asleep on a Wool Cloud](../cases/felted-fox-asleep-on-a-wool-cloud-8c8d1e4b.md)).
- **Light cone.** A pool of light with a visible edge in a dark scene. "under a single cone of warm lamplight" ([Analyst Facing a Mountain of Postponed Letters](../cases/analyst-facing-a-mountain-of-postponed-letters-c9d145b5.md)).
- **Long shadows.** Low sun, usually late in the day. "Emphasize long afternoon shadows" ([06 Minimalist Scooter Village](../cases/06-minimalist-scooter-village-2f97d52a.md)).
- **Light from inside.** A glowing object lit from within. "Warm golden light glows from inside the box" ([Paper Light-Box Village at Dusk](../cases/paper-light-box-village-at-dusk-db4a7a9e.md)).

## Lens and camera

- **Shallow depth of field.** Only a thin slice of the scene is sharp. "shallow depth of field" ([Blond Man Crosses the Morning Flower Market](../cases/blond-man-crosses-the-morning-flower-market-d2d3b695.md)).
- **Sharp subject, soft falloff.** Name what is sharp and what softens. "tack-sharp on hands and moon, softening into blue darkness beyond" ([Girl Holding a Fallen Moon in a Tidal Pool](../cases/girl-holding-a-fallen-moon-in-a-tidal-pool-65b8e726.md)).
- **Macro.** A close view of a small scene. "cinematic macro photograph from a low oblique three-quarter angle" ([Ancient Sword Bridges a Canyon of Pages](../cases/ancient-sword-bridges-a-canyon-of-pages-0a39debe.md)).
- **Motion blur.** Blur along a movement while one part stays crisp. "A subtle motion blur sweeps along the outer curve of the turn while the head stays crisp" ([Glass Whale Carrying Light Fish](../cases/glass-whale-carrying-light-fish-a539a581.md)).
- **Three-quarter view.** The subject turned between front and side. "three-quarter studio photograph" ([Cream Terminal That Never Existed](../cases/cream-terminal-that-never-existed-b3d4a9d3.md)).
- **Overhead view.** Camera straight above the subject. "The viewpoint is directly overhead" ([Goldfish Swims Beneath a Broken Moon Reflection](../cases/goldfish-swims-beneath-a-broken-moon-reflection-e7df7e9a.md)).
- **Low aerial angle.** Slightly above the scene, looking across it. "seen from a low aerial angle" ([Paper City, Living Canopy](../cases/paper-city-living-canopy-55f0fc0d.md)).
- **Side elevation.** A flat side-on view, as in a technical drawing. "stands in side elevation" ([Copper Heron Machine Hunting in Flooded Terraces](../cases/copper-heron-machine-hunting-in-flooded-terraces-7fff6a56.md)).
- **Extreme close-up.** The face fills the frame. "An extreme close-up portrait" ([Rust Seam Splitting a Gray Painted Face](../cases/rust-seam-splitting-a-gray-painted-face-7cabb9a6.md)).
- **Crop height for people.** Say where the frame ends. "three-quarter waist-up view" ([Blond Man Studies a Rainy City Model](../cases/blond-man-studies-a-rainy-city-model-9acedb62.md)) and "three-quarter knee-up view" ([Red-Haired Woman Tends the Glasshouse Seedlings](../cases/red-haired-woman-tends-the-glasshouse-seedlings-37eb8b40.md)).
- **Output size.** Some prompts state pixels. "Render at 1152 by 2048 pixels." ([Chrome Watering Can Pours a Silver Arc](../cases/chrome-watering-can-pours-a-silver-arc-579c8330.md)).

## Composition

- **Negative space.** Empty area that gives the subject room. "generous untouched paper surrounding the figure" ([Mapmaker Unfolding a City](../cases/mapmaker-unfolding-a-city-e9f8e7a1.md)).
- **Diagonal.** A line that carries the eye across the frame. "body arched in a clean diagonal from fingertips at the upper left to toes at the lower right" ([Torn Paper Swimmer Mid Dive](../cases/torn-paper-swimmer-mid-dive-a960b5d6.md)).
- **Placement by thirds.** "exactly one rust-red radio telescope stands on the left third" ([Radio Telescope Inside a Paper Spiral](../cases/radio-telescope-inside-a-paper-spiral-5279e774.md)).
- **Deliberate cropping.** Ask for the crop so it is not read as a mistake. "cropped by the canvas edges exactly like vintage poster typography" ([Glow Retro Poster of a Bass Player and Boy](../cases/glow-retro-poster-of-a-bass-player-and-boy-6b2eb59a.md)).
- **Scale contrast.** A large and a small figure side by side. "Playful scale contrast between the tall figure and the small child" ([Glow Retro Poster of a Bass Player and Boy](../cases/glow-retro-poster-of-a-bass-player-and-boy-6b2eb59a.md)).
- **Focal hierarchy.** Say what the eye should find first and leave room for it. "so the hoop and thread remain the focal hierarchy" ([Silk Thread River](../cases/silk-thread-river-5e0f485f.md)).
- **Exact counts.** Numbers make a scene checkable. "exactly two islands of meaning" ([Analyst Facing a Mountain of Postponed Letters](../cases/analyst-facing-a-mountain-of-postponed-letters-c9d145b5.md)).
- **Depth layers.** Stacked planes that recede. "mountain ridges receding in six distinct depth layers" ([Paper Light-Box Village at Dusk](../cases/paper-light-box-village-at-dusk-db4a7a9e.md)). The review could not count six layers with certainty, so treat large counts as a hint, not a guarantee.

## Material

- **Gloss against matte.** Name the finish of each surface. "crisp material definition between gloss paint, matte carbon and rubber" ([Mint Concept Motorcycle in a Dark Garage](../cases/mint-concept-motorcycle-in-a-dark-garage-8b5688cf.md)).
- **Chrome as a mirror.** Describe what it reflects. "Treat the chrome as a flawless but physically believable curved mirror" ([Chrome Watering Can Pours a Silver Arc](../cases/chrome-watering-can-pours-a-silver-arc-579c8330.md)).
- **Glass and refraction.** "Refraction bends the darkness through the curved body" ([Glass Whale Carrying Light Fish](../cases/glass-whale-carrying-light-fish-a539a581.md)).
- **Impasto.** Thick paint that keeps its brush and palette-knife marks. "painted in cracked gray impasto" ([Rust Seam Splitting a Gray Painted Face](../cases/rust-seam-splitting-a-gray-painted-face-7cabb9a6.md)).
- **Needle felting.** Wool shaped with a barbed needle. "faint needle-puncture texture" ([Felted Fox Asleep on a Wool Cloud](../cases/felted-fox-asleep-on-a-wool-cloud-8c8d1e4b.md)).
- **Glaze pooling.** Glaze that gathers in recesses. "glossy black glazes pool subtly along sculpted contours" ([Sema Becomes a Glazed Ceramic Portrait](../cases/sema-becomes-a-glazed-ceramic-portrait-337c92bd.md)).
- **Forged metal.** Visible hammer marks and joints. "gentle hammer facets and rivets at the joints" ([Iron Dragonfly on Reeds Slice](../cases/iron-dragonfly-on-reeds-slice-2900cbdd.md)).
- **Paper edges.** Torn, deckled or knife-cut, each reads differently. "visible paper grain and knife-cut edges" ([Paper Light-Box Village at Dusk](../cases/paper-light-box-village-at-dusk-db4a7a9e.md)). Also "layered deckled paper fibers" ([Radio Telescope Inside a Paper Spiral](../cases/radio-telescope-inside-a-paper-spiral-5279e774.md)).
- **Gouache.** Opaque water-based paint with a matte finish. "soft rounded gouache shapes with gentle grain and visible brush texture" ([Cub Sending Off a Paper Boat](../cases/cub-sending-off-a-paper-boat-d16dc431.md)).
- **Pressed botanicals.** "natural irregular petal edges, fine veining, delicate layered lift" ([Pressed Flower Wren on Twig Slice](../cases/pressed-flower-wren-on-twig-slice-4712aff7.md)).
- **Paper quilling.** Paper strips set on edge. "the visible paper width of the strips" ([Quilled Blossom Branch with Birds](../cases/quilled-blossom-branch-with-birds-7cd0690d.md)).

## Print

- **Halftone.** Dots that build tone from a few inks. "Coarse halftone dot texture covers every color area" ([Canal Bridge Screenprint Slice](../cases/canal-bridge-screenprint-slice-1919a777.md)).
- **Misregistration.** Color layers that sit slightly off each other. "the color layers sit with a slight charming misregistration" ([Canal Bridge Screenprint Slice](../cases/canal-bridge-screenprint-slice-1919a777.md)).
- **Limited inks.** A fixed number of colors. "printed in only four inks" ([Canal Bridge Screenprint Slice](../cases/canal-bridge-screenprint-slice-1919a777.md)).
- **Print grain.** Texture that makes flat shapes feel printed. "flat vector-like shapes with subtle print grain" ([Glow Retro Poster of a Bass Player and Boy](../cases/glow-retro-poster-of-a-bass-player-and-boy-6b2eb59a.md)). Also "worn screen-print grain, slightly imperfect registration" ([Silk Thread River](../cases/silk-thread-river-5e0f485f.md)).
- **Woodblock print.** "Japanese color woodblock-style print" ([Goldfish Swims Beneath a Broken Moon Reflection](../cases/goldfish-swims-beneath-a-broken-moon-reflection-e7df7e9a.md)).
- **Crosshatch.** Shading built from crossing lines. "fine pen-and-ink crosshatch portrait" ([Red Crosshatch Portrait of an Old Astronomer](../cases/red-crosshatch-portrait-of-an-old-astronomer-fca9cc5e.md)).
- **Engraved plate.** "vintage engraved plate meets modern poster" ([Red Crosshatch Portrait of an Old Astronomer](../cases/red-crosshatch-portrait-of-an-old-astronomer-fca9cc5e.md)).
